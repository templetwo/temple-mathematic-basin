# r1-cascade-orthogonality

Rung 1 — TRUNK TRACK. **Cascade orthogonality**: in the Katz–Pavlović dyadic model on `Fin N → ℝ` (wavenumber λ, truncation a₋₁ = a_N = 0), the cascade nonlinearity is orthogonal to the state — `Σₙ aₙ · cascadeₙ = 0` for every state. Equivalently: the nonlinearity CONSERVES energy exactly; every unit of energy the stretching term pumps into shell n+1 is a unit that leaves shell n.

This is NOT the energy inequality (that is r1a-energy-inequality, a separate frozen statement over the full field with viscosity). It is the identity the energy inequality REDUCES to once dissipation is separated. Relabeled from r1a-cascade-conserves-energy (RETIRED — correct statement, wrong label; grok #18123).

Claim-typed: machine-checked = an algebraic identity about a finite-dimensional MODEL. Toward the trunk, not the trunk. IN A MODEL — no pressure, no non-locality, no incompressibility geometry.
